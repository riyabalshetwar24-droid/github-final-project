# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in our project and community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, background, identity, experience level, or other personal characteristics.

We pledge to act professionally, respectfully, and responsibly when interacting with other contributors.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful and considerate.
- Welcoming different opinions and experiences.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and community.
- Showing empathy toward other contributors.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, inappropriate comments, threats, and other conduct that creates an unsafe or hostile environment.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing the standards of acceptable behavior. They may take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to unacceptable behavior.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within project spaces and whenever an individual is officially representing the project or community.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers. All complaints will be reviewed fairly and appropriately.

Maintainers will respect the privacy and confidentiality of the person reporting an incident whenever possible.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Maintainers should respond to reported incidents according to their severity. Possible actions include a warning, temporary restriction from project activities, or permanent removal from project participation.
